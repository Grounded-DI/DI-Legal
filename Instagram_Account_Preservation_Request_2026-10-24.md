# A Request to Meta: Preserve an Instagram Account’s Photos

**Date:** October 24, 2026  
**Instagram:** [@ked86_](https://www.instagram.com/ked86_/)

An adult Instagram user, Kate, had her account disabled today after being asked to complete facial verification.

She completed the requested verification, but Instagram subsequently disabled her account and displayed a notice stating that her information would be permanently deleted and that no further review was available.

The account contains thousands of personal photographs and years of memories.

It is possible that an automated age-verification process made a mistake. We do not know, and we are not assuming that it did.

We are simply hoping someone at Meta can take another look.

## Requests

1. Preserve the account’s photos and information while the issue is reviewed.
2. Explain why the account was disabled.
3. Allow Kate to verify her identity through another method.
4. Provide a way to retrieve her photographs, even if the account cannot be restored.

We understand why platforms need strong protections for children and why automated enforcement is necessary at scale.

We also hope there is a practical way to correct an occasional mistake.

This is simply an effort to recover important memories. If anyone at [@Meta](https://www.instagram.com/meta/) can direct this to the appropriate team, we would appreciate the help.

Thank you.
